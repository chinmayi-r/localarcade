# M-O first-slice orchestration proof

This synthetic, non-production proof covers the currently safe M-O subset:

- matching M-A hardware/request validation;
- exact propagation of unavailable, blocked and error producer states;
- immediate plain-DTO composition into M-H;
- retained frozen-session exact detail and same-family quantization alternatives;
- canonical creation of the approved non-authorizing runner handoff and U28
  bundle;
- import purity and absence of domain algorithm, UI, network, filesystem,
  process or Tauri dependencies.

The sibling focused proofs cover U29 import inspection, U30 context
composition, permission/inventory orchestration and provisional
verification-plan output binding. They do not prove a real candidate universe
under U27, the complete Rust M-J preparation-input adapter, verification
execution, progress/stop/result, or production surface wiring.
